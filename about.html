Our Story

AKBAR JEWELLERS – Faiz e Hussaini is a name of trust, quality, and elegance in the heart of Lahore. Located at New Canal Park, Ali Plaza Tajbagh, we have been serving our valued customers with pure gold, silver, diamond, kundan, and antique jewellery.

Our journey began with a simple promise – to provide 100% original, hallmarked jewellery at honest and transparent prices. Today, we are proud to be known as the experts in Diamond aur Kundan Antique Jewellery.

Every piece of jewellery at AKBAR JEWELLERS is crafted with love, precision, and dedication. From traditional bridal sets to modern designs, from pure gold coins to elegant diamond rings – we offer a wide range of collections to suit every occasion and every taste.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make every customer's special moment even more memorable with our exquisite jewellery collection. We believe in:

  • ✨ 100% Original Jewellery – Hallmark certified, purity guaranteed
  • 💎 Diamond & Kundan Experts – Antique jewellery specialists
  • 🤝 Transparent Pricing – No hidden charges, ever
  • 💍 Custom Design – Your choice, your style, your size
  • 🚚 Safe Delivery – Delivered with complete secrecy & care
