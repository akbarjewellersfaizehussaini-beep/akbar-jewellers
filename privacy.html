🔒 Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to SHAH REHAN JEWELLERS. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you visit our website or make a purchase.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you place an order:

  • Personal Information: Name, WhatsApp number, email address, delivery address, city
  • Payment Information: Transaction ID (TID), payment screenshot (via Easypaisa)
  • Order Details: Products ordered, karat, weight, size, price
  • Technical Data: Browser type, IP address, pages visited (for analytics)

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for:

  • Processing and delivering your order
  • Sending order confirmation via WhatsApp and Email
  • Verifying payment and preventing fraud
  • Customer support and complaint resolution
  • Website analytics and improvements

4. Data Protection & Confidentiality

We take your privacy very seriously. Your personal information is:

  • Never sold to third parties
  • Never shared with anyone without your consent
  • Securely stored in encrypted databases (Supabase)
  • Kept confidential – your order details, designs, and personal info remain private

Our tagline says it best: "آپ کا آرڈر رازداری کے ساتھ آپ تک پہنچایا جائے گا" – Your order will be delivered with complete confidentiality.

5. Payment Information

All payments are made via Easypaisa (03266501314 – Syed Ahmed Bilal). We only collect:

  • Transaction ID (TID)
  • Payment screenshot (for verification only)

6. WhatsApp & Email Communication

By placing an order, you agree to receive:

  • Order confirmation messages via WhatsApp
  • Email invoices (if email provided)
  • Delivery updates

7. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses cookies and analytics tools to improve user experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings if you prefer.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:
