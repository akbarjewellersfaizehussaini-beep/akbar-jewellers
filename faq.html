Frequently Asked Questions

What is the making charge? +
Making charges are 15% of the gold value. This is clearly shown in the price breakdown when you calculate the price on any product.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)? +
No, we do not offer Cash on Delivery. Minimum 85% advance payment is required (100% default). The remaining 15% is payable on delivery.
How do I make payment? +
Payment is via Easypaisa: 03266501314 (Account Title: Syed Ahmed Bilal). After payment, share the Transaction ID (TID) and screenshot on our WhatsApp or through the checkout page.
Can I customize my jewellery? +
Yes! You can select your preferred karat (24K to 8K), weight (grams, tola, masha, ratti), and size. Every order is custom-made to your specifications.
How long does delivery take? +
Since every piece is custom-made, production takes 7 days. Then delivery: 1-2 days in Lahore, 3-5 days for other cities in Pakistan.
What is your return policy? +
Returns are accepted within 7 days. A 25% deduction applies (for making charges, polish, and custom work). The remaining 75% is refunded.
Is the gold certified? +
Yes, 100%. Every piece of gold and silver jewellery is hallmarked and comes with a purity certificate.
What if I am not satisfied with the product? +
We are committed to your satisfaction. Contact us immediately via WhatsApp at 0092 326 6501314 and we will resolve any issue within 24 hours.
