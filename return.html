🔄 Return Policy

⚠️ Important: 25% deduction on all returns within 7 days. No returns after 7 days.

واپسی پر 25٪ کٹوتی ہوگی – صرف 7 دن کے اندر

1. Return Window

You may return your purchased jewellery within 7 days of receiving the order. Returns after 7 days will not be accepted.

2. Return Deduction (25%)

All returns are subject to a 25% deduction from the total amount. This deduction covers:

  • Making Charges (15%): Custom labor and craftsmanship
  • Polish Charges (10%): Finishing and polishing work
  • Custom Order Impact: Since every piece is custom-made to your specifications, the return cannot be resold at full value

Example: If you purchased jewellery for Rs 100,000, upon return you will receive Rs 75,000 (after 25% deduction).

3. Condition for Return

To be eligible for return, the jewellery must be:

  • In original condition – unworn, undamaged, unaltered
  • With original packaging and certificate
  • Accompanied by the original invoice
  • Not damaged by customer (no scratches, dents, or alterations)

4. Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Custom-engraved jewellery (with names, dates, or personal messages)
  • Artificial/Imitation jewellery (different policy applies – contact us)
  • Gold/Silver coins (investment items – once sold, no return)
  • Damaged items due to customer mishandling

5. Return Process

To initiate a return:

  • Step 1: Contact us on WhatsApp: 0092 326 6501314
  • Step 2: Provide your Order Number and reason for return
  • Step 3: Send photos of the item (unworn, original condition)
  • Step 4: After approval, ship the item back to us
  • Step 5: Once received and inspected, refund will be processed within 3-5 business days

6. Refund Method

Refunds will be issued via:

  • Easypaisa (same number used for payment, if possible)
  • Bank Transfer (if Easypaisa is not available)

Refund will be processed after inspection of the returned item. Cash refunds are not available.

7. Gold Rate Fluctuation

Return value will be calculated based on the current gold rate at the time of return, not the rate at the time of purchase. If the gold rate has decreased since your purchase, the return value will be adjusted accordingly.

8. Exchange Policy

Instead of return, you may also exchange your jewellery for another piece of equal or higher value. Exchange is subject to:

  • Same 7-day window
  • 25% deduction on the original purchase value
  • Additional payment if new item is higher value

9. Damaged or Wrong Item Received

If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, please contact us immediately (within 24 hours) with photos. We will arrange a free replacement or full refund (no deduction) in such cases.
